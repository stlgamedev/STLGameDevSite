The St. Louis Game Developer Community is going to the Game Developers Conference in San Francisco from March 1st through the 4th, 2027! The event includes an expo, networking events, awards shows, and a variety of tutorials, lectures, and roundtables by industry professionals on game-related topics covering programming, design, audio, production, business, and visual arts.

If you would like to go to GDC this year with us, **[apply for a ticket here](https://discord.com/channels/406541601750515718/1564664699440861345/1569039297263304874)**! We want to give those that have never been before the chance to connect with game developers across the country and beyond! This is your chance to network, share your games, and represent the St. Louis community at various summits, meetups, and parties!

If you go on STLGameDev's behalf, you must share your experiences that you had with the community when you return in the form of a Microtalk on the STLGameDev Discord!

If you don't want to wait around, [secure your pass](https://gdconf.com/) and join us! You can get 10% off your pass today with the promo code: **CP-BPEwu**. There are a lot of different pass types this year that are much cheaper as well, such as a pass for the Festival Hall only for $175.
